Experiment 1 · 7408 AND gate

Augmented Reality View

Drop the virtual breadboard into your real room. The circuit auto-cycles the AND truth table so you can watch the output LED respond.

  • Android Chrome + ARCore - tap Launch AR, aim at a flat surface, then tap the floor/table to place the board.
  • iPhone Safari - gyro view. Move the phone to look around the circuit.
  • Desktop Mouse - drag to rotate around the circuit.
