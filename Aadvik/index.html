Experiment No. 3

AIM: To implement HALF ADDER & FULL ADDER circuits using basic logic gates.

APPARATUS USED:

  • Digital IC Logic Trainer Kit (Model DTK-101 with Regulated +5V DC Supply & Toggle Input Switches)
  • Bread Board (830 Point Solderless MB-102 Mounted Bay)
  • Hookup Wires / Jumper Wires (Red, Black, Yellow)
  • LEDs (Glowing Red for Sum, Glowing Green for Carry)
  • ICs 7486 (Quad 2-Input XOR), 7408 (Quad 2-Input AND), 7432 (Quad 2-Input OR)

THEORY:

An adder is a digital logic circuit in electronics that implements addition of numbers. In many computers and processors, adders are used to calculate memory addresses, implement arithmetic operations within ALU and determining table indices. Furthermore, adders are employed in various other parts of the processor to handle tasks like incrementing counters, managing loop indices, and accessing elements in arrays. These can be built for many numerical representations like excess-3 or binary coded decimal. Adders are classified into two types: half adder and full adder. The half adder circuit has two inputs: A and B, which add two input digits and generate a Carry and Sum as output. The full adder circuit has three inputs: A, B and C, which add the three input numbers and generates a Carry and Sum.

PROCEDURE:

  1. Mount the ICs (7486, 7408, and 7432 for Full Adder) across the central notch on the Breadboard.
  2. Connect DC Power (+5V & GND) from the Digital IC Trainer Kit Power Terminals to the breadboard power rails using Red & Black wires.
  3. Connect patch cords and Yellow jumper wires from the Trainer Kit Input Switches (SW-1: A, SW-2: B, SW-3: Cin) to the corresponding IC input pins.
  4. Apply input logic combinations (A, B, Cin) by flipping the Trainer Kit SPDT Toggle Switches.
  5. Observe output results on the Glowing Red (Sum) & Green (Carry) LEDs and verify with the provided truth table.

RESULTS:

With the help of Digital Logic Trainer Kit (DTK-101) and 7486 (XOR), 7408 (AND) and 7432 (OR) ICs, we successfully implemented the HALF ADDER & FULL ADDER circuits and verified their outputs with the given truth-tables.

POSSIBLE SOURCES OF ERROR:

  • Wire connections may be improper or loose.
  • Patch-cord connections may have short circuits.
  • Damaged or malfunctioning logic gate IC.
  • Incorrect IC orientation (pin 1 notch alignment).

PRECAUTIONS:

  • ICs should be placed in the middle of bread-board bridging the notch.
  • All wires should be tightly connected.
  • Do not exceed +5V DC power supply to avoid IC damage.
  • Verify power & ground connections before turning power supply ON.
